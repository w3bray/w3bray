**Apex Hunter | Red Team Operator & Exploit Developer**

> *"In the deep shadows of the machine, where the architecture of reality is written in binary and memory addresses, we find the true blood of the system. Fear the old code."*

Operating at the absolute edge of cybersecurity and systems architecture. I do not merely write code; I dissect it, reverse-engineer its flaws, and bend the infrastructure to my will. My focus lies in **Ring 0/Kernel-Level Engineering**, **Bare-Metal Exploitation**, and **Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) Simulation**. Where traditional developers see a secure application, I see an intricate web of memory leaks, buffer overflows, and architectural oversights waiting to be shattered.

As a **Gray Hat**, I understand the rules of engagement. As a **Black Hat** in the simulation, I break them to expose the fragile illusions of digital security. I specialize in bypassing EDRs, crafting untraceable payloads, and lateral movement through heavily fortified networks. The Red Team is not just a profession; it is a relentless hunt for the Zero-Day.

Fluent in the ancient, low-level languages that govern the machine's soul, I manipulate the matrix from the ground up—from raw CPU instructions in **Assembly (x86-64)** and memory management in **C/C++**, to sophisticated full-stack architecture and automated infiltration scripts.
